# 3D-LEX access request — unsent draft

To: o.ranum@surrey.ac.uk

Subject: Access to 3D-LEX ASL motion data for an avatar research prototype

Hello Dr. Ranum,

I am working on SIGN, an accessibility research prototype with a rigged avatar.
Our current procedural motions use ASL-LEX descriptors and consensus-gated
ASL-Phono estimates. They remain explicitly unverified; neither source provides
the complete recorded performances needed for reliable avatar signing.

Could you provide access to the 1,000-sign ASL portion of 3D-LEX, including body,
hand and facial capture, and the alignment to ASL-LEX glosses?

It would help to receive the export formats, skeleton/joint definitions,
coordinate system and units, frame rates, synchronization/timecode information,
facial channel definitions, calibration/rest poses and known recording gaps.
Please also confirm the applicable CC BY 4.0 terms and attribution, and whether
retargeted motion assets may be redistributed with a research prototype.

We plan to retarget to a Rocketbox avatar and have qualified Deaf ASL signers
review the exact rendered motions before presenting them as validated signing.

Thank you.
